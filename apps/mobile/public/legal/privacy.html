CampusFlow

Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

CampusFlow is a student-built planner made as a university course project. It is not an official app of any university and is not endorsed by one. This page explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, display name, time zone and a scrypt hash of your password. Your password itself is never stored. Email addresses are not verified.
  • What you save in the app: tasks and their steps, courses, assessments and grades, classes, coursework and exams, study plans, focus sessions, routines and their check-ins, saved campus events, reminders and notification preferences.
  • Optional wellness check-ins: mood, energy and stress ratings and notes, only if you choose to save them.
  • Calendar files you import: sent to our server, read once to suggest classes, and then discarded. Only the classes you choose to add are saved.
  • Security records: session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes, and session records stay until you delete your account. To slow abuse, sign-in, sign-up, password-confirmation and calendar-import attempts are counted against hashes of your email address, your IP address or your account. These counters expire within an hour.

We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party SDKs that collect your data. The web app keeps your session in memory only. It is gone when you close the tab.

Why we store it

Only to run the app for you: building your daily plan, syncing between your devices, reminding you, and protecting accounts from abuse. We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on a server rented from Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland, and is sent only over HTTPS. Your phone or browser also keeps a copy of your own data so the app opens quickly and works offline. Signing out or deleting your account clears it. In a web browser, the copy is also cleared the next time CampusFlow opens without you signed in, but on shared computers always sign out before you leave.

Campus events come from public university calendars that the server downloads. Nothing about you is sent to those calendars.

How long we keep it

  • Your data stays until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Deleting your account removes your data from the live database immediately.
  • Daily database backups are deleted after about 15 days, so deleted data leaves the backups within roughly two weeks.
  • The app's API requests are not access-logged. The web server's access log records IP address, time, browser type and the pages loaded (never what you save) and is kept for about 14 days. Server error logs may record an IP address when a request fails.
  • A demo account (demo@example.com) is shared and reset every night. Don't save anything personal in it.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Settings → Account & privacy → Prepare my copy.
  • Correct it: edit anything you saved directly in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings → Account & privacy → Review account deletion. This is permanent.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device settings.

For anything else, see Support.

Children

CampusFlow is meant for university and college students. Please don't use it if you are under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
