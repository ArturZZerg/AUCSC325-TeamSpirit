Support

Start in the app

Open Help from the sign-in screen or Settings. It answers common questions about Today, classes, coursework, focus sessions, reminders and working offline, and it works without a connection.

Your data

  • Download everything you saved: Settings → Account & privacy → Prepare my copy.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Account & privacy → Review account deletion.
  • Forgot your password? Email reset isn't available, so the account can't be recovered. You can create a new one.

Report a problem or suggest an idea

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Issues there are public, so describe what happened without including passwords, your email address or personal details.

Reminders not arriving?

  • Allow notifications for CampusFlow in your device settings, and check that the reminder is on in Settings.
  • Reminders are scheduled on each device. Open the app once on a new device so it can set them up.
  • Battery-saving modes can delay notifications on some phones.
