# CampusFlow — product definition

## Target user

An undergraduate at University of Alberta Augustana (Camrose) who has five or so
courses, a part-time job or team commitments, and coursework spread across
Canvas, syllabi and their own notes. They plan on their phone, between classes.

## The three core problems

1. **"What do I need to do today?"** Deadlines, classes, personal errands and
   campus events live in different places. CampusFlow builds one Today plan from
   all of them, so the student doesn't have to put it together each morning.
2. **"Am I going to be ready?"** Students see deadlines too late to prepare.
   CampusFlow turns coursework and exams into scheduled study sessions, fits
   them around classes and shows progress and grades against targets.
3. **"I keep forgetting."** Time-sensitive things slip by. Reminders, a morning
   overview and the next-class status make sure the important item shows up at
   the right moment.

## Why open it daily

The Today screen answers the question at a glance: next class, what's due, what's
planned and what's on at campus tonight. Checking things off, logging focus time
and keeping routine streaks give a small daily payoff without gamification for
its own sake.

## Principles

- Not a Canvas clone. Academic data is just one input to the plan.
- Honest data: show saved/stale/unavailable states rather than invented content.
- Offline-first reads; the server stays authoritative.
- No university credentials, logos or implied official endorsement.
